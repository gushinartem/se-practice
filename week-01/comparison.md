6. Reflection 


AI has really sped up the construction and planning of a program. It can take on a lot of routine work that would normally take me a significant amount of time to do manually. In my experience, it didn’t really cost me extra time. Almost everything worked from the first prompt, and the results were mostly correct. Because of this, I could focus more on the general idea and structure of the program instead of spending hours on small and repetitive tasks.However, I still don’t completely understand what the question means by “artefacts”. I’m not sure what exactly is supposed to be included in this term in the context of software development. I would probably need some additional explanation or an example to understand what is expected.I also think that a human engineer must always be responsible for checking everything that AI produces. AI can make mistakes, misunderstand requirements, or generate code that looks correct but has hidden problems. This can become especially dangerous when developing software for areas such as healthcare or the military. In these fields, even a small bug or incorrect decision can cause serious damage, harm people, or even lead to someone’s death. AI is a very useful tool, but it should support engineers rather than completely replace their responsibility.
